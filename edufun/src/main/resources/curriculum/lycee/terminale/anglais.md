# Unit 1 — Global issues

## TN-AN-01 | Linking words and complex sentences | 55 min
Objectif : Organiser un texte argumentatif en anglais.
### Je retiens
= Addition: furthermore, in addition — Contrast: although, whereas, nevertheless
= Cause: because of, due to — Consequence: therefore, as a result, consequently
### Je m'exerce
1. Join with « although »: « It was raining. They played the match. »
### Corrigé
1. Although it was raining, they played the match.

## TN-AN-02 | Technology and society | 55 min
Objectif : Débattre des effets de la technologie.
### Je retiens
= artificial intelligence, social media, digital divide, cybercrime, e-learning, mobile money
= Technology has made our lives easier; however, it raises privacy concerns.
### Je m'exerce
1. Write two advantages and one drawback of mobile money in Côte d'Ivoire.
### Corrigé
1. It allows people without bank accounts to send money easily. It is fast and safe. However, users can be victims of fraud.

# Unit 2 — The BAC exam

## TN-AN-03 | BAC reading comprehension | 60 min
Objectif : Maîtriser les types de questions de l'épreuve écrite.
### Je retiens
> Types : True/False with justification, multiple choice, find words meaning…, answer questions in your own words, reference questions (« what does "they" refer to? »). Read the questions first, then scan the text.
### Je m'exerce
1. What is a reference question?
### Corrigé
1. A question asking what a pronoun or word in the text refers to.

## TN-AN-04 | BAC essay writing | 60 min
Objectif : Rédiger un essai argumenté de 150 à 200 mots.
### Je retiens
> Introduction (topic + opinion) — body paragraphs (one idea each, with example) — conclusion (summary + recommendation). Use varied vocabulary and linking words; check tenses and agreement.
### Je m'exerce
1. Plan an essay: « Should young Africans stay in Africa or go abroad? »
### Corrigé
1. Introduction; paragraph 1: reasons to go abroad (studies, experience); paragraph 2: reasons to stay (build the continent, family, opportunities); conclusion: go to learn, come back to contribute.
