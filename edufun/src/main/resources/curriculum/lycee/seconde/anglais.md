# Unit 1 — Youth and society

## 2N-AN-01 | Talking about habits: used to | 55 min
Objectif : Exprimer des habitudes passées.
### Je retiens
= I used to walk to school, but now I take the bus.
= Did you use to play football? — Yes, I did.
### Je vérifie
? Quelle phrase exprime une habitude passée qui n'existe plus ?
+ I used to walk to school.
- I am used to walk to school.
- I use to walking to school.
! « used to + base verbale » exprime une habitude passée.
? Kouamé demande à son ami s'il jouait au football quand il était enfant. Il dit :
+ Did you use to play football?
- Did you used to play football?
- Do you used to play football?
! Après « did », on emploie « use to » sans -d.
### Je m'exerce
1. Write two sentences about what you used to do when you were a child.
### Corrigé
1. Example: I used to play with marbles. I used to sleep at my grandmother's house.

## 2N-AN-02 | Relative clauses | 55 min
Objectif : Utiliser who, which, that, whose, where.
### Je retiens
= The girl who won the prize is my cousin. The book which I read was interesting.
= The man whose car was stolen called the police. This is the town where I was born.
### Je vérifie
? Quel pronom relatif remplace une personne sujet ?
+ who
- which
- where
! « who » s'emploie pour les personnes ; « which » pour les choses.
? Complète : « This is the town … I was born. » (Abidjan)
+ where
- whose
- who
! « where » renvoie à un lieu.
? Complète : « The man … car was stolen called the police. »
+ whose
- who
- which
! « whose » exprime la possession.
### Je m'exerce
1. Join: « This is the teacher. She helped me. »
### Corrigé
1. This is the teacher who helped me.

# Unit 2 — Health and environment

## 2N-AN-03 | Modals: should, must, might, could | 55 min
Objectif : Exprimer conseil, obligation et probabilité.
### Je retiens
= You should drink clean water. (advice) — Drivers must wear a seat belt. (obligation)
= It might rain tonight. (possibility) — He could be at home. (possibility)
### Je vérifie
? Quel modal exprime une obligation ?
+ must
- might
- could
! « must » exprime l'obligation ; « might » et « could », la possibilité.
? Pour donner un conseil contre le paludisme, on dit :
+ You should sleep under a treated mosquito net.
- You might sleep under a treated mosquito net.
- You could to sleep under a treated mosquito net.
! « should » sert à donner un conseil.
### Je m'exerce
1. Give advice to prevent malaria.
### Corrigé
1. You should sleep under a treated mosquito net and you should remove stagnant water.

## 2N-AN-04 | Writing an argumentative paragraph | 60 min
Objectif : Défendre une opinion en anglais avec des connecteurs.
### Je retiens
= Firstly, … Secondly, … Moreover, … However, … In conclusion, …
= In my opinion, … I believe that … On the one hand … on the other hand …
### Je vérifie
? Quel connecteur introduit une idée opposée ?
+ However
- Moreover
- Firstly
! « However » marque l'opposition ; « Moreover » ajoute un argument.
? Pour terminer un paragraphe argumentatif, on écrit :
+ In conclusion, …
- Secondly, …
- On the one hand, …
! « In conclusion » annonce la fin de l'argumentation.
### Je m'exerce
1. Write a paragraph: « Should mobile phones be allowed at school? »
### Corrigé
1. Example: In my opinion, phones should be allowed with rules. Firstly, they help students do research. Moreover, parents can reach their children. However, they can distract students. In conclusion, phones are useful if teachers control their use.
