# Unit 1 — Plans and future

## 4E-AN-01 | Going to and will | 55 min
Objectif : Exprimer des projets et des prédictions au futur.
### Je retiens
= going to: plans already decided — I'm going to visit my uncle next week.
= will: decisions at the moment, predictions — It will rain tomorrow. I'll help you.
### Je m'exerce
1. Complete: « Look at the clouds! It … (rain). »
### Corrigé
1. is going to rain.

## 4E-AN-02 | Making suggestions and invitations | 45 min
Objectif : Proposer, inviter, accepter et refuser.
### Je retiens
= Let's go to the beach! — Why don't we play football? — Would you like to come to my party?
= Yes, I'd love to. / Sorry, I can't. I have to study.
### Je m'exerce
1. Invite a friend to your birthday party and write his answer.
### Corrigé
1. Would you like to come to my birthday party on Saturday? — Yes, I'd love to!

# Unit 2 — Experiences

## 4E-AN-03 | The present perfect | 55 min
Objectif : Parler d'expériences et d'actions récentes.
### Je retiens
= have / has + past participle: I have visited Yamoussoukro. She has never seen snow.
= ever, never, already, yet, just
> Past simple = moment précis terminé (yesterday). Present perfect = sans moment précis ou lien avec le présent.
### Je m'exerce
1. Complete: « Have you ever (eat) … pizza? »
### Corrigé
1. eaten.

## 4E-AN-04 | Health and the body | 50 min
Objectif : Décrire des symptômes et donner des conseils avec should.
### Je retiens
= I've got a headache / a stomachache / a fever / a cold.
= You should see a doctor. You shouldn't eat too much sugar.
### Je m'exerce
1. Give advice to a friend who has malaria symptoms.
### Corrigé
1. You should go to the health centre and you should sleep under a mosquito net.

# Unit 3 — Rules and obligations

## 4E-AN-05 | Must, have to, mustn't | 50 min
Objectif : Exprimer l'obligation et l'interdiction.
### Je retiens
= You must wear your uniform. Students have to arrive at 7:30. You mustn't cheat in exams.
> « mustn't » = interdiction ; « don't have to » = pas d'obligation.
### Je m'exerce
1. Write two school rules with must / mustn't.
### Corrigé
1. You must respect teachers. You mustn't use your phone in class.

## 4E-AN-06 | The environment | 50 min
Objectif : Parler des problèmes environnementaux.
### Je retiens
= pollution, deforestation, waste, recycle, plant trees, plastic bags, global warming
= We should recycle plastic. People cut down too many trees.
### Je m'exerce
1. Write three sentences about how to protect the environment in your town.
### Corrigé
1. Example: We should plant trees. We shouldn't throw waste in the street. We can recycle bottles.
