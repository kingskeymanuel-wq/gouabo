# Unit 1 — Plans and future

## 4E-AN-01 | Going to and will | 55 min
Objectif : Exprimer des projets et des prédictions au futur.
### Je retiens
= going to: plans already decided — I'm going to visit my uncle next week.
= will: decisions at the moment, predictions — It will rain tomorrow. I'll help you.
### Je vérifie
? Pour une prédiction ou une décision prise sur le moment, on utilise :
+ will
- going to
- have to
! Will exprime une décision immédiate ou une prédiction.
? Choisis la bonne phrase pour un projet déjà décidé :
+ I'm going to visit my uncle in Korhogo next week.
- I going to visit my uncle in Korhogo next week.
- I will to visit my uncle in Korhogo next week.
! Going to exprime un projet déjà décidé : be + going to + verbe.
### Je m'exerce
1. Complete: « Look at the clouds! It … (rain). »
### Corrigé
1. is going to rain.

## 4E-AN-02 | Making suggestions and invitations | 45 min
Objectif : Proposer, inviter, accepter et refuser.
### Je retiens
= Let's go to the beach! — Why don't we play football? — Would you like to come to my party?
= Yes, I'd love to. / Sorry, I can't. I have to study.
### Je vérifie
? Pour accepter une invitation, on dit :
+ Yes, I'd love to.
- Sorry, I can't.
- Why don't we?
! « Yes, I'd love to » signifie oui, avec plaisir.
? Comment invites-tu Fatou à ta fête ?
+ Would you like to come to my party?
- Do you like come to my party?
- Would you come like to my party?
! Would you like to + verbe sert à inviter poliment.
### Je m'exerce
1. Invite a friend to your birthday party and write his answer.
### Corrigé
1. Would you like to come to my birthday party on Saturday? — Yes, I'd love to!

# Unit 2 — Experiences

## 4E-AN-03 | The present perfect | 55 min
Objectif : Parler d'expériences et d'actions récentes.
### Je retiens
= have / has + past participle: I have visited Yamoussoukro. She has never seen snow.
= ever, never, already, yet, just
> Past simple = moment précis terminé (yesterday). Present perfect = sans moment précis ou lien avec le présent.
### Je vérifie
? Le present perfect se forme avec :
+ have / has + participe passé
- had + verbe à l'infinitif
- will + participe passé
! Present perfect : have ou has suivi du participe passé.
? Complete: « She has never … snow. »
+ seen
- saw
- see
! Après has, on met le participe passé : seen.
### Je m'exerce
1. Complete: « Have you ever (eat) … pizza? »
### Corrigé
1. eaten.

## 4E-AN-04 | Health and the body | 50 min
Objectif : Décrire des symptômes et donner des conseils avec should.
### Je retiens
= I've got a headache / a stomachache / a fever / a cold.
= You should see a doctor. You shouldn't eat too much sugar.
### Je vérifie
? Pour donner un conseil, on utilise :
+ should
- would
- did
! You should see a doctor : should exprime le conseil.
? Your friend has got a fever. What do you say?
+ You should see a doctor.
- You should eat too much sugar.
- You shouldn't rest.
! On conseille de consulter un médecin en cas de fièvre.
### Je m'exerce
1. Give advice to a friend who has malaria symptoms.
### Corrigé
1. You should go to the health centre and you should sleep under a mosquito net.

# Unit 3 — Rules and obligations

## 4E-AN-05 | Must, have to, mustn't | 50 min
Objectif : Exprimer l'obligation et l'interdiction.
### Je retiens
= You must wear your uniform. Students have to arrive at 7:30. You mustn't cheat in exams.
> « mustn't » = interdiction ; « don't have to » = pas d'obligation.
### Je vérifie
? « You don't have to wear a tie » signifie :
+ ce n'est pas obligatoire
- c'est interdit
- c'est obligatoire
! Don't have to = absence d'obligation ; mustn't = interdiction.
? Quelle phrase exprime une interdiction à l'école ?
+ You mustn't cheat in exams.
- You must wear your uniform.
- You don't have to cheat in exams.
! Mustn't exprime l'interdiction.
### Je m'exerce
1. Write two school rules with must / mustn't.
### Corrigé
1. You must respect teachers. You mustn't use your phone in class.

## 4E-AN-06 | The environment | 50 min
Objectif : Parler des problèmes environnementaux.
### Je retiens
= pollution, deforestation, waste, recycle, plant trees, plastic bags, global warming
= We should recycle plastic. People cut down too many trees.
### Je vérifie
? What does « deforestation » mean?
+ cutting down too many trees
- planting new trees
- recycling plastic bags
! La déforestation est la destruction des forêts.
? Quelle phrase propose de protéger l'environnement à Abidjan ?
+ We should recycle plastic bottles.
- We should throw waste in the street.
- We should cut down more trees.
! Recycler le plastique protège l'environnement.
### Je m'exerce
1. Write three sentences about how to protect the environment in your town.
### Corrigé
1. Example: We should plant trees. We shouldn't throw waste in the street. We can recycle bottles.
