# Unit 1 — Hello!

## 6E-AN-01 | Greetings and introductions | 50 min
Objectif : Saluer, se présenter et présenter quelqu'un en anglais.
### Situation d'apprentissage
A new student from Ghana arrives in your class. The teacher asks you to welcome her in English.
### Je retiens
= Hello! / Hi! / Good morning! / Good afternoon! / Good evening!
= What's your name? — My name is Awa. / I'm Awa.
= How are you? — I'm fine, thank you. And you?
= This is my friend Koffi. — Nice to meet you!
= Goodbye! / See you tomorrow!
> « Good night » ne sert que pour dire bonne nuit avant d'aller dormir, pas pour saluer.
### Je m'exerce
1. Complete: « … is your name? — My … is Yao. »
2. Translate: « Bonjour, je m'appelle Aya. Enchantée ! »
### Corrigé
1. What ; name.
2. Good morning, my name is Aya. Nice to meet you!

## 6E-AN-02 | The verb « to be » | 50 min
Objectif : Conjuguer et utiliser le verbe « to be » au présent (affirmatif, négatif, interrogatif).
### Je retiens
= I am (I'm) — you are (you're) — he / she / it is (he's) — we are — they are
= Negative: I am not, he is not (isn't), they are not (aren't)
= Question: Are you a student? — Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
### Je m'exerce
1. Complete: « She … my sister. They … in Form 1. »
2. Make negative: « He is from Man. »
### Corrigé
1. is ; are.
2. He isn't from Man.

# Unit 2 — My family and my home

## 6E-AN-03 | My family | 50 min
Objectif : Présenter les membres de sa famille avec le possessif.
### Je retiens
= father, mother, brother, sister, grandfather, grandmother, uncle, aunt, cousin
= my, your, his, her, our, their — Koffi's sister (la sœur de Koffi)
### Je m'exerce
1. Translate: « La mère de Yao est infirmière. »
2. Complete: « Awa is my … ; her brother is my … (mon cousin) »
### Corrigé
1. Yao's mother is a nurse.
2. cousin ; cousin.

## 6E-AN-04 | Numbers, age and telephone numbers | 45 min
Objectif : Compter de 1 à 100 et donner son âge et un numéro.
### Je retiens
= one, two, three … ten, eleven, twelve, thirteen … twenty, thirty, forty, fifty … one hundred
= How old are you? — I'm eleven (years old).
= What's your phone number? — It's 07 08 …
### Je m'exerce
1. Write in letters: 15 ; 40 ; 99.
### Corrigé
1. fifteen ; forty ; ninety-nine.

# Unit 3 — At school

## 6E-AN-05 | Classroom objects and instructions | 45 min
Objectif : Nommer les objets de la classe et comprendre les consignes.
### Je retiens
= a pen, a pencil, a ruler, an eraser, a book, an exercise book, a schoolbag, a board
= Open your books. Close the door. Listen. Repeat. Stand up. Sit down.
> On utilise « an » devant un son voyelle : an eraser, an orange.
### Je m'exerce
1. Complete with a or an: … umbrella ; … ruler.
### Corrigé
1. an umbrella ; a ruler.

## 6E-AN-06 | Telling the time and days of the week | 50 min
Objectif : Dire l'heure et les jours de la semaine.
### Je retiens
= What time is it? — It's eight o'clock. / It's half past ten. / It's a quarter to six.
= Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday
### Je m'exerce
1. Say in English: 7 h 30 ; 9 h 15.
### Corrigé
1. It's half past seven ; it's a quarter past nine.

# Unit 4 — Daily life

## 6E-AN-07 | The simple present: daily routine | 55 min
Objectif : Décrire ses habitudes au présent simple.
### Je retiens
> Au présent simple, on ajoute -s à la 3e personne du singulier : I wake up → she wakes up. Négation : don't / doesn't + verbe ; question : Do / Does … ?
= I get up at six. She goes to school by bus. He doesn't eat meat. Do you play football?
### Je m'exerce
1. Complete: « My father (work) … in a bank. »
2. Ask a question: « You like attiéké. »
### Corrigé
1. works.
2. Do you like attiéké?

## 6E-AN-08 | Food and drinks | 45 min
Objectif : Exprimer ses goûts alimentaires avec like / don't like.
### Je retiens
= rice, bread, fish, chicken, eggs, plantain, yam, beans, fruit, water, juice, milk
= I like rice. I don't like eggs. What's your favourite food? — My favourite food is foutou.
### Je m'exerce
1. Write two sentences about what you like and don't like.
### Corrigé
1. Example: I like grilled fish. I don't like milk.
