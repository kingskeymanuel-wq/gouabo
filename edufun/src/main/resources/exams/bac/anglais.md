# Méthode
> Reading comprehension, language skills and an essay (about 150–200 words).
1. Read the questions before the text; underline key words.
2. True/False: justify with a quotation. Reference questions: identify what the pronoun refers to.
3. Answer « in your own words » when asked: do not copy the text.
4. Essay: introduction (topic + opinion), two body paragraphs with examples, conclusion; use linking words.
5. Keep 10 minutes to check tenses, agreement and spelling.

# Sujet type 1 — Youth unemployment
Text:
In many African countries, young people represent the majority of the population. Every year, thousands of graduates enter the job market, but there are not enough jobs for all of them. Some decide to create their own businesses: they sell clothes online, develop mobile applications or transform local products. Governments have launched programmes to support young entrepreneurs, but access to funding remains difficult. Experts believe that training in digital skills and agriculture could create millions of jobs.
1. True or false? Justify. a) Young people are a minority. b) Some graduates create their own businesses.
2. What do young entrepreneurs do? Give two examples.
3. What remains difficult for them?
4. Essay (150 words): « What can young Ivorians do to fight unemployment? »
## Corrigé
1. a) False: « young people represent the majority of the population ». b) True: « Some decide to create their own businesses ».
2. They sell clothes online and develop mobile applications (or transform local products).
3. Access to funding remains difficult.
4. Marking guide: introduction with a clear opinion; two developed ideas (training in digital skills and agriculture, entrepreneurship supported by microcredit and mobile money); examples from Côte d'Ivoire; conclusion; correct tenses and linking words.
