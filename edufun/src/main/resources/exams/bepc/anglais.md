# Méthode
> The exam tests reading comprehension, grammar and vocabulary, and a short piece of writing.
1. **Read the questions first**, then read the text and underline the key words.
2. **True or False** : always justify with a short quotation from the text.
3. **Answer in full sentences** in English, using the words of the question.
4. **Grammar** : check the tense (present, past, perfect), the 3rd person -s, and irregular verbs.
5. **Writing** : respect the number of words, use linking words (first, then, however, finally) and check your spelling.

# Sujet type 1 — A school trip to Grand-Bassam
Text:
Last Saturday, the pupils of Form 4 went on a school trip to Grand-Bassam, the first capital of Côte d'Ivoire. They visited the National Museum of Costume, where they saw traditional clothes from all the regions of the country. After lunch, they walked along the old colonial houses. Their teacher explained that the historic town has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2012. In the evening, the pupils were tired but happy. "I will never forget this day," said Aïcha.
**I. Reading comprehension (8 points)**
1. Where did the pupils go? (1 pt)
2. True or false? Justify. a) Grand-Bassam was the first capital of Côte d'Ivoire. b) The pupils visited a zoo. (3 pts)
3. What did they see at the museum? (2 pts)
4. Since when has Grand-Bassam been a UNESCO World Heritage Site? (2 pts)
**II. Language (6 points)**
1. Put into the passive: « UNESCO listed the town in 2012. » (2 pts)
2. Report Aïcha's words: Aïcha said that… (2 pts)
3. Give the past form of: go, see, say, walk. (2 pts)
**III. Writing (6 points)**
Write a short letter (60–80 words) to a friend about a trip you enjoyed.
## Corrigé
**I. Reading comprehension**
1. They went to Grand-Bassam.
2. a) True: « Grand-Bassam, the first capital of Côte d'Ivoire ». b) False: « They visited the National Museum of Costume ».
3. They saw traditional clothes from all the regions of the country.
4. Since 2012.
**II. Language**
1. The town was listed by UNESCO in 2012.
2. Aïcha said that she would never forget that day.
3. went – saw – said – walked.
**III. Writing — marking guide**
Greeting and closing (Dear…, Best wishes), past tense used correctly, at least two details about the trip, linking words, 60–80 words.
